peerdash

A local-first infinite canvas for dashboards, synced peer to peer. A chatbot can join a canvas as one more peer.

For AI agents: you cannot read a canvas over HTTP. The canvas is not on this server, and the part of a share link after # is not sent to the server. To make or join a canvas, read https://yellowcap.github.io/peerdash/llms.txt. It tells you how to install the peerdash plugin (with your user's approval) and how to call join with the share link. The full guide is at https://yellowcap.github.io/peerdash/index.md.
